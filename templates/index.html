OUR HEART

A generation with hope and purpose.

Wings For Christ believes every young person deserves to know their life has value and possibility. Through school outreach, college programs, music and encouragement, we inspire students to discover their purpose and potential.

01

OUR VISION

Young people living with hope and purpose.

To raise a generation that doesn't just survive but thrives. We envision young people discovering their identity, recognizing their worth, and reaching their full potential. We are committed to adding value to those who feel discouraged, forgotten, or overlooked, helping them find hope, purpose, and the confidence to make a difference in the world.

02

OUR MISSION

Encouraging the next generation.

To empower the next generation through music, creative arts, education, mentorship, and community outreach. We create opportunities for young people to discover their talents, develop life skills, overcome challenges, and grow into confident, responsible, and compassionate individuals.
